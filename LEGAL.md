## YouTube and yt-dlp

Downloading a video is not automatically legal. Copyright, contractual, and other rights may apply even when a video is publicly visible. YouTube's Terms of Service generally prohibit downloading content except through features YouTube authorizes. Using yt-dlp may therefore breach those terms and lead to account restrictions or technical blocking.

OmniForge does not bypass DRM, paywalls, age verification, region locks, login restrictions, or other access controls. Do not add or use such bypasses. OmniForge is not affiliated with YouTube and does not grant any rights to YouTube content.

## Media and File Tools

- Converting, compressing, trimming, joining, extracting audio, making GIFs, and creating clips do not grant rights to the source material. Only process media you are authorized to use.
- Metadata stripping can remove copyright-management information. Do not remove or alter copyright notices, attribution, or other rights information from files you do not own or have permission to modify.
- PDF and archive tools should be used only with documents and archives you are authorized to access and modify. Do not use them to forge documents or conceal unauthorized access.
- Encryption is lawful for lawful data. Do not use it to conceal illegal material. Strong encryption may be subject to export-control rules when distributing software internationally.
- Secure Delete permanently overwrites and removes files. Use it only on data you own or are authorized to destroy. Never use it to destroy evidence, data under a legal hold, or data belonging to someone else. SSDs, snapshots, backups, and cloud sync may retain copies.
- Speech transcription and dictation can involve wiretap, privacy, and consent laws. Record or transcribe conversations only when you have the required consent and legal authority.
